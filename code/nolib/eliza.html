About
ジャーナル (jānaru) is an offline local-first privacy engine designed for deep personal journaling with the active presence of an alternate perspective listening to your thoughts. It functions purely locally to serve as a private space to speak, reflect, and trace personal history.
  • Doctor: Eliza structural Rogerian psychotherapist script loops.
  • Big Sis: High volume attentive listening state model.
  • Your Cat: Feline dialogue simulation tracking contextual parameters.
  • Spirits around you: Stochastic contextual Markov transition network.
