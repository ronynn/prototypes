ABOUT

夜駆け / NIGHT RUNNER is an endless rooftop runner. Jump the gaps, dodge the shadow, ride the day.

CONTROLS — Tap the screen, or press SPACE / ↑ / W to jump. Hold longer for a higher jump.

SCORING — Distance is measured in metres. Land within 34 pixels of a rooftop edge for a near-miss bonus. Every 450 m the shadow wakes up and tries to shove you off balance.

WORLD — Day cycles every 700 m. Wind gusts hit every 400 m. Rain falls constantly.

RECORDS — Best and daily best are saved locally.
